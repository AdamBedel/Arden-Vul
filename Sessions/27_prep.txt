It is the 26th of Fidios. Coming up are the "Mercedonian Days" aka the days of wages. It is a time for staying indoors
rectifying expenses and wages. Upon finishing, Archontean's celebrate by polar plunging into the river and eating caramel taffy. 
- The point of the tradition is that the working men finish with their expenses / taxes much sooner, and get several days to celebrate 
and relax. 


Greeted by Sneeze "yous hasn't forgotten about your best pal, sneeze, has ya doggies??" He is the newly appointed gambler to King Wauskeenim,
and will be taking over for pebble-toucher. he escorts the party to the wet caves, where Killik joins the retinue.
